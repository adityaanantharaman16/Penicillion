## Next work

1. **Local corpus pilot.** Start from the shipped release, build the held-out answer set, measure current coverage, then prepare a reviewed open-pool candidate. Save the inventory, examples of corrected misses, unsafe near-matches and repeat simulations with the release. The larger editorial milestones can continue independently of hosting.
2. **Local hosting preparation.** When implementation is requested, apply the schema and frozen edition to isolated Supabase-compatible Postgres, adapt HTTP and shared limits, and test two actors across cold starts. Verify Daily/Practice timers, replay, private saves and the daily calendar. [Hosting plan](HOSTING-PLAN.md)
3. **Relay prototype and parity.** Prove one private two-player match with authorized reconnects and unattended timeouts. Then cover Quick match, larger rooms, spectators, rematches and failure recovery. Record results against the current local baseline. [Slide Relay plan](SLIDE-RELAY-MIGRATION.md)
4. **Staging and launch decisions.** Present a current quote, Supabase tier and backup proposal for staging approval. After approval, validate real callbacks, Realtime revocation, restore and rollback, and 30 mixed simultaneous players. Public launch and DNS require separate approval.
