## Representative challenge cases

| Prompt | Accepted | Retry or reject | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human cell | RBC, red blood cell, erythrocyte | mitochondrion, platelet, PC | The accepted forms denote the same whole cell. The other entries are an organelle, a fragment or an ambiguous abbreviation. |
| Human cell structure | mitochondrion | chloroplast, astrocyte | The prompt specifies human subcellular structures. |
| Central glial cell | astrocyte, oligodendrocyte | Schwann cell, neuron | Schwann cells are peripheral glia. Neurons fall outside the glial scope. |
| CNS disorder | multiple sclerosis, syringomyelia | peripheral neuropathy | The brain, spinal cord and meninges define the stated scope. |
| Handheld surgical instrument | scalpel, rongeur, Richardson retractor | scalpel blade, suture, gauze | Separate supplies fall outside the instrument scope. |
| Named cranial nerve | optic nerve | optic disc, optic chiasm | Distinct structures remain distinct. |
| Numbered clotting factor | factor V, factor VII | factor VI | VI is retired. The exact number matters. |
| Antibody class | IgG | IgG1, kappa | Subclasses and light chains fall outside the five heavy-chain classes. |
| Standard amino acid | aspartate, Asp | A | One-letter guesses are outside the displayed scope. |
