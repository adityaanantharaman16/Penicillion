# Original Night Culture reveal

This [short capture](night-culture.gif) records the first local design. For “Name an instrument used in surgery.” the Gigli saw earned 100 points, then labelled Atlas Plate. Two tall stems bent together above the new colony, with coral fruit at the crown. The fullscreen revision calls this award “Are you a doctor?” and follows its growing filament into the dish.
