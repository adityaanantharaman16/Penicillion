# Night Culture

The bench is drawn on a 152-row pixel grid. A night window, glass jars, drawer unit, stained slides and open notebook occupy the sides of the wide room. Narrow frames retain the keeper and dish. A tall portrait viewport reveals more wall and the front of the bench. The prompt, score, controls and descriptions are real browser text.

## Poses

1. On the landing screen, a modest mature sample under the lamp suggests what the keeper grows. The ink and sea-glass frame carries the specimen room name and a dated daily issue.
2. On each prompt, the central plaque holds the question. Seven marks above it and seven numbered specimen wells on the front rail record progress. The input and timer live in the paper dock below the illustrated bench.
3. Each locked answer has a fixed location on the agar. A 10-point answer settles into a silver-green island. At 15 points it gains a satellite bud, and at 30 a short blue-tipped runner grows. For 60, 85 and 100 points, the outer bench dims and the camera travels with one filament. A 60-point find opens into a low lilac fan, an 85-point find grows an amber canopy, and a 100-point find raises an arch of coral buds and a three-tipped crown. In Daily and Practice the typing tray rises into place at the bench edge. An accepted term rises in its tier color before the rarity takes its place. Smaller colonies grow over about two seconds. Rare structures open over three to four seconds as spores lift from the tip, and the camera returns to the full bench in about five seconds. Every completed structure remains in its original place through later results. The player can advance immediately.
4. The recap keeps the finished culture and filled wells above a seven-entry specimen log. Reduced motion and static mode paint the final shape at once. All point totals, category labels, result text and progress marks are available to assistive technology.

The three streak cosmetics change rim pigment, attach a sea-glass specimen label or add a coral spore print. Their selection is local to the browser. The server holds earned milestones. They do not affect rounds or scores.

The keeper strip comes from the drawing functions in the game. Run `node web/design/render-sprites.mjs` to regenerate [the four poses](sprites.svg). Font licenses and source details are in [TYPE.md](TYPE.md). No reference screenshots or third-party illustrations ship with the game.

Sound is generated by local oscillators after a gesture. A small award has a short glass-like interval, larger scores add an ascending tail, recap layers four sustained notes, and a relay handoff plays two short notes. Mute and volume are remembered. Device audio support is optional.
